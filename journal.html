Writing

Journal

This is a research journal exploring physics and the use of AI as a daily tool for scientific work. I want to experiment with forms of scientific work and communication outside the traditional paper format. My goal is to rapidly iterate through ideas, hopefully many of them new, using AI as a tool for work and as a source of rapid feedback. Each entry is largely written by me, with annotations when a section is partly AI written. There are also AI reports that can aid the reader in judging the correctness of entries. Nevertheless, I'm sure there will be errors; I hope to correct these while maintaining the provenance of the entries.
