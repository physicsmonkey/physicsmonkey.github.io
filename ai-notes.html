AI Notes

Notes on artificial intelligence itself: the "physics" of neural networks, how they represent and compute, and how we might understand and align them. These are study projects rather than research results. Each one works slowly through a paper or essay, fills in the background a physicist would want, and re-creates the key claims in the smallest setting that keeps the phenomenon. They are written with AI assistance, and the code and executed results are kept alongside the text so that every figure can be rerun.
