Calculation 1: finite-N operator-ensemble tests

This folder contains an exact-diagonalization test of the coupling-ensemble versus operator-ensemble statements in ../couplings-vs-ops.md.

syk_operator_ensemble_ed.py uses the Jordan-Wigner representation of Majorana strings, stored compactly as phase-weighted permutations of the computational basis. It samples the p=4 SYK Hamiltonian, computes thermal one-point functions xi(a) = tr(rho mu(a)) for every Majorana string of a chosen weight, and then measures

  • the fixed-sample operator average M1 = average_a xi(a),
  • the fixed-sample operator second moment M2 = average_a xi(a)^2,
  • the empirical disorder covariance/correlation between different strings,
  • the empirical excess kurtosis of each string over the disorder ensemble.

The main run used in the entry is

/home/physicsmonkey/projects/levy-syk/.venv/bin/python syk_operator_ensemble_ed.py --n-values 8 10 12 --weights 4 --beta 1 --samples 120 --coupling-scale 4 --seed 20260607 --outdir outputs_beta1

The larger-size runs reported in the entry are

/home/physicsmonkey/projects/levy-syk/.venv/bin/python syk_operator_ensemble_ed.py --n-values 16 --weights 4 --beta 1 --samples 80 --coupling-scale 4 --seed 20260608 --outdir outputs_beta1_N16
/home/physicsmonkey/projects/levy-syk/.venv/bin/python syk_operator_ensemble_ed.py --n-values 18 --weights 4 --beta 1 --samples 60 --coupling-scale 4 --seed 20260609 --outdir outputs_beta1_N18

The high-statistics run used for the final table and figure is

/home/physicsmonkey/projects/levy-syk/.venv/bin/python syk_operator_ensemble_ed.py --n-values 8 10 12 14 16 18 --weights 4 --beta 1 --samples 1000 --coupling-scale 4 --seed 20260610 --outdir outputs_beta1_highstats

The comparison figure is generated by

/home/physicsmonkey/projects/levy-syk/.venv/bin/python plot_theory_comparison.py --summary outputs_beta1_highstats/summary.csv

The factor --coupling-scale 4 simply makes the finite-size thermal one-point functions large enough to measure cleanly at these small Hilbert-space dimensions. It does not affect the dimensionless self-averaging ratios reported in the entry.

An additional lower-temperature run,

/home/physicsmonkey/projects/levy-syk/.venv/bin/python syk_operator_ensemble_ed.py --n-values 8 10 12 --weights 4 --beta 4 --samples 80 --coupling-scale 4 --seed 20260606

is stored in outputs/. It gives the same qualitative result for M1, but noticeably larger finite-size fluctuations in M2.
